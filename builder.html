📘 Official CV Formatting Instructions

Balaji Institute of Telecom & Management (BITM) • Career Planning & Placement Cell Guidelines

  1. Name: 14 pt, bold, and properly aligned as per the given format.
  2. Font: Use Arial throughout the CV.
  3. Font Size: Keep the overall CV content at 11 pt.
  4. Headings: Only the headings should be bold. Keep the details/content in normal font.
  5. Alignment: Follow the alignment given in the CV format. Keep tick marks (✓) and other elements properly center-aligned wherever required.
  6. Margins: Keep a little space on all four sides of the page.
  7. Spacing: Maintain proper and consistent spacing between sections. Keep equal space after every section and avoid unnecessary blank spaces.
  8. Specialisation: Write your specialisation as per the given format. If it doesn’t fit, use a short form. Tech students should use the required short form.
  9. Internship/Certification Dates: Mention the internship and certification dates on the right side, aligned consistently throughout the CV.
  10. Passing Year: Mention only the passing year. Example: 2023 ✅ | 2022–23 ❌. For MBA, mention 2026–28 in the Passing Year column and write “Pursuing” in the Percentage/CGPA column.
  11. Marks: If you are adding marks, mention them only in Percentage or CGPA. Do not use any other format.
  12. Photo: Use a formal photograph with a white background.
  13. Content: Fill in your correct and updated details. Do not change the order or add unnecessary information.
  14. Formatting: Do not change the overall structure, headings, or layout of the given CV format.
  15. Final Check: Check spelling, contact details, font size, bold headings, alignment, spacing, dates, marks, photo, and overall appearance before submitting.
