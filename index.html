📘 Official CV Formatting Instructions

Corporate Relations & Placement Cell, Balaji Institute of Telecom & Management (BITM)

1
Candidate Name 14 pt, bold, and properly aligned as per the given format.
2
Uniform Typography Use Arial font throughout the CV without exception.
3
Standard Font Size Keep the overall CV body content strictly at 11 pt.
4
Heading Bolding Only headings are bold. Keep details and content in normal font.
5
Alignment & Checkmarks Follow table alignment. Keep tick marks (✓) center-aligned.
6
Balanced Margins Maintain balanced margins on all four sides of each page.
7
Section Spacing Maintain equal space after every section; avoid blank lines.
8
Specialisation Format Write your specialization as per format; use official short forms if needed.
9
Dates on Right Side Mention internship and certification dates right-aligned consistently.
10
Passing Year Standard Passing year: 2023 ✅ | 2022–23 ❌. For MBA, write 2026–28 & "Pursuing".
11
Marks Representation Mention marks only in Percentage or CGPA. No other format.
12
Formal Photograph Use a formal passport-size photograph with a clean white background.
13
Content Order Fill updated details. Do not alter section sequence or add extra sections.
14
Structural Integrity Do not alter the overall structure, table borders, or header banners.
15
Final Verification Check Review spelling, contact numbers, dates, and director signatures before submit.
